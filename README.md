# Kandy Cinematic Weddings

Create a polished, premium, fully responsive website CONCEPT for the Sri Lankan business “Kandy Wedding Planners” (Kandy, Sri Lanka). This is an independent speculative concept by Source Code Software Solutions, NOT an official client website and not commissioned/endorsed by the business. Keep this disclosure clear but tasteful in the footer and a small visible notice near the top: “Independent concept design — not the official website.” Do not imply that this business hired us.

GOAL: demonstrate how a high-quality wedding planner website could help generate inquiries from couples and their families.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Premium editorial wedding aesthetic, warm ivory background, muted champagne gold, deep burgundy accents, soft charcoal text, elegant serif display typography paired with clean sans-serif body type.
- Large, cinematic, realistic Sri Lankan wedding imagery as illustrative stock/AI concept visuals only; label the portfolio gallery “Illustrative concept imagery — not verified work by this business.” No fake reviews, fake awards, fake client logos, fake stats, invented dates, invented price packages, or assertions about the business beyond its name, category, and Kandy location.
- Subtle tasteful animations, refined whitespace, beautiful mobile layout, accessible contrast, strong responsive behaviour. Avoid tacky gold gradients, excessive cards, and generic template appearance.

PAGE STRUCTURE:
1. Premium hero with headline “Your Day. Your Story. Beautifully Planned.” subtitle about thoughtful wedding planning in Kandy; two CTAs “Start an Enquiry” and “Explore the Gallery.” Mark this as proposed demo copy, not confirmed official copy.
2. A visual experience/approach section (planning, coordination, personal details) described as illustrative proposed positioning.
3. Services grid with proposed categories clearly marked for owner confirmation: Full Wedding Planning; Wedding-Day Coordination; Venue & Decor Coordination; Traditional Sri Lankan Ceremony Coordination; Bridal Party & Guest Coordination; Vendor Coordination. Never imply these exact services are confirmed by the real business.
4. Portfolio/gallery of beautiful Sri Lankan wedding concepts. Ensure caption says imagery is illustrative only and not actual client work.
5. A simple 3-step “How planning could work” section: Share your vision; shape the details; celebrate with confidence.
6. A thoughtful FAQ with non-binding, general questions and answers; avoid specifics about business availability or prices.
7. An inquiry form (name, email/phone, event date optional, venue/location optional, service interest, message). Demo only; do not connect to a database or pretend submissions are stored. On submit show clear message: “This is a concept demo. Your details have not been sent.”
8. Contact section showing public listing phone 077 461 6306 only with note that all contact details must be verified by the business before any real launch. Do not invent a WhatsApp link or email.
9. Footer with “Independent concept design by Source Code Software Solutions” and “This is not the official Kandy Wedding Planners website. Images and proposed copy are illustrative.”

Avoid invented testimonials, fake Google review badges, unsupported claims, or saying the business lacks an official website. Keep it elegant and premium, a credible sales-demo asset.
